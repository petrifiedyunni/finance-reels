Finance for Divas

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 15, 2026

What this app is

Finance Reels runs on your computer. There is no Finance for Divas cloud account. We do not operate a public website that collects visitor emails or ads.

What stays on your computer

  • Rendered videos and captions in your local generated/ folder
  • TikTok access and refresh tokens in .tiktok/token.json after you log in
  • API keys you put in your local .env file

Those files are not uploaded to us. Do not commit them to git.

What TikTok receives

When you choose to publish, the app sends TikTok the video file and caption through TikTok’s official APIs. TikTok then processes that content under TikTok’s Privacy Policy.

Login Kit also lets TikTok share a basic profile identifier (such as open id and display name) with the app so it knows which account you connected.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data
  • We do not run third-party ads in this app
  • We do not clone voices of real people

Optional third-party keys

If you add xAI, ElevenLabs, or OpenAI keys, those providers process the prompts or audio you send them under their own policies. That is optional. The studio works offline without those keys.

Your choices

Disconnect TikTok in TikTok settings. Delete .tiktok/token.json to drop the local token. Delete generated/ to remove rendered videos.

Contact

Use the email on your TikTok for Developers account.
